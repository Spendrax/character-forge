Character Forge: privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Character Forge is a free, personal app for building tabletop role-playing characters (D&D 5e, Pathfinder 2e, Mutants & Masterminds 3e) and playing at the table. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your characters, notes, house rules, encounters, imported books and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device. They are not sent to us; we have no server that stores them.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive on the Backup page, the app asks Google for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can only create and update its own files: a Character Forge folder in your Drive with your backup files. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

  • The backup goes straight from your browser to your own Google Drive. Nobody else, including the app’s author, receives a copy.
  • Google’s sign-in stays in your browser for about an hour. No password is ever seen by the app.
  • The data is used only to back up and restore your own characters. It is not shared, sold or used for anything else.
  • You can disconnect at any time on the Backup page, or remove the app’s access in your Google Account (Security, Third-party connections). Your backup files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Character Forge’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other optional features

  • Playing at a table: when you join a game, your character sheet and what you share (rolls, notes, messages) go directly to the other players’ devices, not through our servers.
  • Spotify: if you connect Spotify, your login stays in your browser and the app only talks to Spotify to play music.
  • AI helper: if you add your own AI key, it stays in your browser and your questions go only to the AI service you chose.
